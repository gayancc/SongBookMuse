# Information architecture

- **Transport** shows what is loaded (LCD) and drives the set: previous, perform, next, metronome, auto-scroll, tuner, settings.
- **Browser** has Songs and Setlists. Songs offers All songs, Favorites and user folders, then sort and the song list. Setlists expand into ordered steps (songs and notes) with tools: edit, add note, shuffle, duplicate, print, delete.
- **Sheet** is the score with a tool strip: lyrics only, two columns, favorite, song details, actions (edit, add to setlist, export, print, delete).
- **Song details** (inspector) holds pitch, notation, view, section order, annotations, folders, lock and details.
- **Settings** holds appearance, chords (notation, key spelling, flat preference), auto-scroll delay, backup and MIDI.
