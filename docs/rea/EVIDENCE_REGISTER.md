## Status vocabulary

**VERIFIED** denotes behavior or a format fact reproduced or established within a named evidence boundary. **DOCUMENTED** indicates official source support. **INFERRED** indicates an interpretation awaiting corroboration. **UNKNOWN** denotes missing evidence. **BLOCKED** denotes a required resource or environment that is unavailable for the specific check.
